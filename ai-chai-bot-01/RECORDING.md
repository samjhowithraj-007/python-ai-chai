# Part 1 — face-cam script (about 30 seconds)

Record on the webcam, same spot and light as the Agentic AI episode. Look at the lens.
Pause 1 second before you start and after you finish. Retakes are fine: just say the line again.
Don't worry about exact timing; the video is re-timed to match your speech.

Your room is removed in the edit and replaced with the video's own dark background, so you blend into the scene.
For a clean cut-out: a plain wall behind you, light on your face (window or lamp in front, not behind),
a bright or coloured T-shirt (not black, it would merge into the dark background), and keep your head and
shoulders inside the frame.

| Scene on screen | Say this |
|---|---|
| 1. Hook: "Chai ka chatbot banao!" + bot face (0–4 s) | "Chai ka chatbot banate hain! Milo Chai Bot se." |
| 2. Chat window demo (4–11 s) | "Chatbot kya hai? Ek program jo aapse baat kare. Aap poocho 'menu?', bot bole 'Masala, Adrak, Elaichi!'" |
| 3. Bas 3 cheezein (11–16 s) | "Iske liye bas teen cheezein chahiye: input se suno, print se jawab do, aur while True se chalte raho." |
| 4. Code + terminal + "buddhu" stamp (16–24 s) | "Yeh raha pehla Chai Bot, sirf chaar lines. Lekin dekho, kuch bhi poocho, bolta hai 'Chai ready!'. Abhi thoda buddhu hai!" |
| 5. 50-part roadmap + website + subscribe (24–30 s) | "Pachaas parts mein ise asli AI agent banayenge. Notes aur code samjhowithraj.online pe. Part 2 mein if-else. Follow karo!" |
