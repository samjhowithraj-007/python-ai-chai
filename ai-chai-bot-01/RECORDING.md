# Part 1 — face-cam script, take 2 (about 30 seconds)

Record on the webcam, same spot and light as the Agentic AI episode. Look at the lens.
Pause 1 second before you start and after you finish. Retakes are fine: just say the line again.
Don't worry about exact timing; the video is re-timed to match your speech.

Your room is removed in the edit and replaced with the video's own dark background, so you blend into the scene.
For a clean cut-out: a plain wall behind you, light on your face (window or lamp in front, not behind),
a bright or coloured T-shirt (not black, it would merge into the dark background), and keep your head and
shoulders inside the frame.

| Scene on screen | Say this |
|---|---|
| 1. Hook | "Chai ka chatbot banate hain! Milo Chai Bot se." |
| 2. Chat window | "Chatbot matlab ek program jo aapse baat kare. Aap bolo 'menu', bot bole 'Masala, Adrak, Elaichi!'" |
| 3. Bas 3 cheezein | "Bas teen cheezein: input se suno, print se jawab do, while True se chalte raho." |
| 4. Code + "buddhu" stamp | "Yeh raha pehla Chai Bot, sirf chaar lines. Par kuch bhi poocho, bolta hai 'Chai ready!' Abhi buddhu hai!" |
| 5. Roadmap + website + subscribe | "Pachaas parts mein ise AI agent banayenge. Notes aur code samjhowithraj.online pe. Part 2 mein if-else. Follow karo!" |

Take 2 tips: keep the energy up (smile on the hook line), keep the mic or laptop close to your mouth,
and leave only a short breath between lines. For the fastest edit, record with a green background
(green cloth behind you, or CapCut Remove BG + green background before you send it).

## Talking points (say it your way, don't read)

Glance at a point, look at the lens, and tell it like you'd explain to a friend over chai.
Any wording is fine; the scenes are re-timed to whatever you say.

1. **Hook:** we're making a chai chatbot today → meet Chai Bot.
2. **Chat window:** a chatbot is just a program you talk to → you ask "menu", it replies with flavours.
3. **3 cheezein:** input = listen, print = reply, while True = keep going.
4. **Code:** here's the first Chai Bot, 4 lines → but whatever you ask, it says "Chai ready!" → still buddhu (laugh).
5. **End:** in 50 parts it becomes an AI agent → notes + code on samjhowithraj.online → Part 2: if-else → follow.
