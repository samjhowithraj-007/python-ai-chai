## Intent

Part 1 of the 50-part "AI + Python: chai ka chatbot banao" series for "Samjho with Raj" (plan in
`../ai-chai-bot/SERIES.md`). Introduces Chai Bot: a chatbot is just input (suno) + print (jawab do) +
while True (chalte raho), all things viewers already know from Parts 1–10. The bot is still "buddhu" (same
