10 cup, sirf 2 line! ☕ Python range() | Python Chai Part 15 #shorts

### Description
10 cup chai ka order… 10 baar print likhoge? Nahi! 😅
for + range() = ginti ki machine. 2 line mein 10 cup ready! ☕🐍

📌 Code:
for i in range(1, 11):
    print(f"Cup {i} ready")

print(list(range(5)))         → [0, 1, 2, 3, 4]
print(list(range(2, 11, 2)))  → [2, 4, 6, 8, 10]

range(start, stop, step) · stop wala number kabhi nahi aata!
