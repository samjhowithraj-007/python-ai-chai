## Intent

A short Instagram square post for "Samjho with Raj" that makes Python feel easy by mapping it onto making chai:
ingredients are variables, the recipe is a function, and "jab tak ubaal na aaye" is a while loop. Friendly,
desi, a little playful. Chosen concept from the pitch round: "Chai = Python program".

## Customizations

- On-screen text in romanised Hinglish, no voiceover; works muted.
- Low lo-fi music bed under the whole video.
- At most 4 short code lines on screen at a time, big enough to read on a phone.

## Notes

- Channel colours: cyan for key words, amber for warm accents, dark background.
